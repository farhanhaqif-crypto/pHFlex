University-level explanation

pH is defined as −log[H⁺]. Strong acids fully dissociate, while weak acids dissociate partially. pH electrodes respond based on the Nernst equation and require calibration for accuracy.

Measurement notes

• Calibrate using pH 4, 7, 10 buffers.
• Rinse electrode between samples.
• Let the probe stabilize before reading.
• Use temperature compensation for accurate results.

Example calculations

[H⁺]=1×10⁻³ → pH=3 (acidic)
[OH⁻]=1×10⁻² → pOH=2 → pH=12 (basic)
