University-level explanation

pH represents the negative logarithm of hydrogen ion activity: pH = −log[H⁺]. Strong acids dissociate nearly completely, while weak acids only partially do so. Temperature, ionic strength, and electrode calibration directly influence the accuracy of pH measurements.

Measurement notes

• Calibrate using standard buffer solutions at pH 4, 7, and 10.
• Rinse the electrode between measurements to avoid contamination.
• Ensure stable temperature or compensate for variations.

Example calculations

For [H⁺] = 1×10⁻³ M → pH = 3 (acidic).
For [OH⁻] = 1×10⁻² M → pOH = 2 → pH = 12 (basic).
