pH Classification (this system)

Strong acid: pH ≤ 3
Mild acid: 3 < pH < 7
Neutral: pH = 7
Mild base: 7 < pH < 11
Strong base: pH ≥ 11

University-level explanation

pH is the negative logarithm of hydrogen ion activity: pH = −log[H⁺]. Each unit is a 10× change in [H⁺]. Strong acids (e.g., HCl) fully dissociate in water while weak acids (e.g., CH₃COOH) only partially dissociate. Temperature and ionic strength influence electrode response.

Measurement notes

Calibrate with at least two standard buffers (pH 4 & pH 7 or 7 & 10). Rinse the electrode between samples and allow sufficient stabilization time. For high accuracy, include temperature compensation.

Example calculations

If [H⁺] = 1×10⁻³ M, pH = 3.00. If [OH⁻] = 1×10⁻² M, pOH = 2 ⇒ pH = 12 (strong base).
